01Overnight — Global Events & Sentiment

The Fed hiked its policy rate a quarter point to 3.75–4.00% overnight — the first hike in three years, and a unanimous vote. The dot plot came in hawkish, penciling in one more hike before year-end, and Chair Kevin Warsh described the internal debate as a "good family fight" without giving markets a clear signal on what comes next.

Wall Street had been higher for most of the session but reversed once the decision and press conference landed: the Dow fell about 1.2%, the S&P 500 gave up roughly 0.45%, and the Nasdaq ended little changed. Treasury yields jumped hard — the 10-year near 4.68% and the 30-year past 5.19%, its highest in about two decades — and the move isn't confined to the US; global sovereign yields have been climbing alongside it.

Geopolitics is the other big overnight thread: the US–Iran conflict escalated further, with the US Vice President signaling the war moves into "a new phase" within a month and the Pentagon acknowledging a munitions shortage. Saudi Arabia reportedly cancelled crude cargoes after a pipeline closure, and explosions were reported inside the kingdom — the combination is what's driving crude sharply higher.

Net read: a hawkish Fed, surging bond yields, and a war-driven oil spike is a classic emerging-market headwind combination — it's the backdrop Indian markets open into today.
